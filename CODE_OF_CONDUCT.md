# Code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers should keep project discussions respectful, useful,
and welcoming. Focus criticism on code and ideas, respect different backgrounds
and experience levels, and accept constructive feedback.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, and publication
of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.
Do not use issues or pull requests for spam or unrelated promotion.

Report concerns privately to ramirez.ruiz.eliezer.reuven@gmail.com with relevant
context. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, ask participants to change
behavior, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports
will be handled with care; share only information needed to understand the concern.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces where participants represent this project.
